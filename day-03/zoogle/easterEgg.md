Zoogle Easter Eggs

I’m Feeling Lucky
Don’t bother clicking it. It’s feeling unlucky and runs away every time.

dark
Turns the lights off. Your eyes say thanks.

light
Turns the lights back on. Sorry about that.

bing
Zoogle turns into Zing. The logo, title and buttons all change, and you get a button that says “Zing Search or Whatever.”

google
Zoogle gently corrects you: did you mean zoogle? Yes, we did.

zixted
Zoogle asks did you mean the goat? Search for the goat and it asks if you meant Zixted. They’re the same thing.

mirror
The whole page flips like you’re reading it in a bathroom mirror. Type unmirror to fix it.

askew
The page tilts like someone bumped the table. Type straight to level it back out.

shake
The logo shakes like it just had way too much coffee.

comic sans
The font changes. Everyone has an opinion about this font.

matrix
Green code rains down behind the page. You’re in.

why
Zoogle answers: well because. That’s all you get.
